# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue containing credentials, customer data, internal addresses, exploitable configuration, or operational evidence.

Report security concerns through the contact method published at:

<https://sarabelinformatika.hu/.well-known/security.txt>

Include the affected path, impact, reproduction conditions, and a safe proof of concept. Remove personal, client, tax, payroll, and production data before sharing evidence.

## Supported scope

Security reports may cover scripts, examples, documentation, or repository configuration. Vulnerabilities in third-party products must also be reported to their maintainers.
