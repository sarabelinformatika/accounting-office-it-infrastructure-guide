# 15 — Compliance, suppliers, and evidence

## Governance

Technical controls support obligations but do not determine them. The office should obtain qualified legal, data-protection, accounting, employment, and contractual advice for applicable requirements.

## Evidence model

For each important control retain:

- policy or approved requirement;
- responsible owner;
- configuration or report;
- collection date and source;
- reviewer and result;
- exception and corrective action;
- retention and access rules.

Redact personal and client data. Store evidence in a protected location separate from the system being evidenced where appropriate.

## Suppliers

Assess application vendors, cloud services, hosting, backup, remote support, internet providers, and managed IT services. Record service scope, data access, authentication, logging, sub-processors where relevant, backup responsibility, incident contact, support hours, exit/export process, and dependency on individual personnel.

## Access reviews

Review staff, external accountant, auditor, vendor, support, guest, service, emergency, and administrative access. Confirm need, owner, MFA, last use, expiry, and revocation path.

## Exceptions

An exception must identify the requirement, reason, affected assets, risk, compensating controls, owner, approval, review date, and expiry. Permanent undocumented exceptions become architecture.
