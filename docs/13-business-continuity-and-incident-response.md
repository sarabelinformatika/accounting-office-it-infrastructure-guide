# 13 — Business continuity and incident response

## Priority services

Rank services by deadline and impact. Payroll, tax filing, client communication, identity, application databases, and document access may require different recovery order and workarounds.

## Minimum continuity record

- business owner and technical lead;
- incident declaration authority;
- contact paths that work without the affected system;
- service RTO and data RPO;
- minimum staffing and equipment;
- manual or alternate workflow;
- dependencies and recovery order;
- supplier escalation;
- communication and evidence requirements;
- return-to-normal criteria.

## Ransomware response

1. Preserve safety and prevent further spread.
2. Isolate affected devices without destroying evidence.
3. Protect backup and identity systems.
4. Disable or contain compromised identities and remote-access paths.
5. Record timeline, indicators, affected assets, and decisions.
6. Determine clean recovery point and rebuild method.
7. Validate identity, endpoint, network, application, and data integrity before reconnecting.
8. Meet contractual, legal, regulatory, insurance, and communication obligations with qualified advice.

## Tabletop exercise

At least annually, simulate a realistic loss of the primary application/file environment during a deadline period. Measure detection, decision time, access to documentation, supplier response, restore duration, data loss, and communication quality.

## Lessons learned

Track corrective actions to owners and dates. An incident is not closed merely because service is restored.
